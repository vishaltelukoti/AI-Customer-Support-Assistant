## Failed payments
When a payment fails at checkout, ask the customer to confirm the billing address, available funds, and whether the card issuer has declined the transaction. The customer can try again after checking those details or use another supported method. Do not ask for a full card number, card security code, password, or one-time code. If several attempts fail, route the order reference to the fictional payments support queue.

## Double charges and authorizations
A pending authorization can appear before a payment settles and may resemble a duplicate charge. Collect the order reference and transaction dates, then request a payment-record review. Avoid promising that a pending authorization has been refunded until the records confirm it. A confirmed duplicate follows the fictional refund policy and is returned to the original payment method after review.
